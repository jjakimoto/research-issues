AgentIdeaBench: Benchmarking Scientific Ideation in the Agent Era

📰 Latest · 2026-09-09 Full Article
Read full article →

Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
Date: 2026-09-09

Source: arxiv · 2026-09-07
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07611

Static 'here is your reading list' ideation benchmarks have saturated at the frontier; letting a model gather its own literature separates models 4.4x better — but the gain is capability-gated and lands in grounding, not measured originality.

Executive summary

AgentIdeaBench evaluates scientific ideation across 40 densely scored subfields in five disciplines under two matched settings: Static (model is handed a curated reference set) and Active (model gets only the subfield name plus a Semantic Scholar SEARCH/FETCH tool under a 10-call budget). 35 models are evaluated, 33 with matched outputs, with headline stats on 28 matched primary-roster models. Hypotheses are scored 1-10 on originality, feasibility, clarity, impact and specificity by a 3-critic open-weight ensemble that must argue originality against date-filtered prior art retrieved at judging time. Active carries 4.4x the between-model variance and ~2.1x the per-year scaling slope; the gain is strongly predicted by static ability (r=+0.69) and improves feasibility/clarity/specificity while leaving measured originality flat.

The problem with static ideation benchmarks

Prior ideation evaluation curates a fixed reference set, has the model read it in one pass, and asks an LLM judge to rate novelty/feasibility. This removes the retrieval loop agentic deployment exercises and loses resolution as models saturate: the eight strongest models sit within 0.39 of a 1.53-point Static span yet span 1.39 under Active.

  • Static measures recombination of supplied references, not evidence gathering
  • Static top flattens from the late-2024 cutoffs onward
  • LLM judges call an idea novel whenever its precedents fall outside training memory

Benchmark design: two matched tracks

100 subfields curated across CS, physics, biology, chemistry and medicine; 40 densely scored with 3 hypotheses per model/subfield/track (~21k critic calls). Both tracks share subfields, an 80-150-word single-paragraph output format, and scoring. Static supplies title+abstract reference sets; Active supplies only the subfield name plus a search/fetch tool under a fixed 10-call budget. Generation at temperature 0.7, seed 42, 6000-token limit.

  • Free-text paragraph rather than a multi-slot template, to avoid box-filling that depresses originality
  • Budget of 10 fixed by a sweep over {1,2,5,10,15,20} calls on five backbones; no backbone gained between 5 and 10 (all p>=0.30)
  • Target literature published after nearly all evaluated models' knowledge cutoffs

Verifier grounding: literature-verified LLM critics, not human experts

Scoring is entirely LLM-as-judge: three open-weight critics at temperature 0, per-dimension trimmed mean dropping the most generous critic, originality-dominant weighting (O:2, I:1.5, F:1, C:0.5, S:0.5). The key control is that date-filtered prior art is retrieved per hypothesis before scoring and the critic must justify originality against it. Human involvement is limited to a small pairwise-agreement study and human-landmark anchoring; no expert novelty study was run.

  • Leakage control: target papers postdate nearly all model cutoffs; an appendix rules out memorization as the explanation for the cutoff trend
  • Robustness: weighting sensitivity, judge ensemble, single-judge, self-grading removal, pairwise agreement
  • Human-landmark anchors score +1.00 to +2.24 above the per-discipline all-model Active average; the CORE-7 award-paper set scores 7.71, above every model's Active mean
  • Authors state the missing and most important validation is an expert study rating novelty directly

Results: separation, capability gate, and where the gain lands

Active totals span 3.20 to 6.33, carry 4.4x the between-model variance of Static, and a bootstrap-SE criterion distinguishes 62% of top-half pairs versus 12% under Static. The gain correlates with static ability at r=+0.69, mean gain only +0.34 with 19/28 improving; the weakest ability quartile loses -0.18 while the strongest gains +0.76.

  • Scaling on knowledge-cutoff year: Static +0.54/year vs Active +1.16/year; interaction +0.62/year, 95% CI [0.41,0.94], permutation p<1e-3
  • Gate replicates out-of-roster on five held-out Gemini models at within-family r=0.88; r=0.57 pooled over all 33
  • Per-dimension: feasibility +1.21, clarity +0.63, specificity +0.58, impact +0.23, originality -0.14 (not significant, sign test p=0.85)
  • Feasibility is highest on a weak model (Llama-4 Maverick 6.87), dissociating from originality/impact

Two controls decomposing the Active bundle

A recall-only (closed-book) track gives only discipline plus subfield name; a replay track refeeds each model, in passive Static format, the exact references its own Active agent surfaced. Static is statistically indistinguishable from closed-book recall (-0.09, p=0.11) while Active beats recall (+0.23, p<1e-3); the content term is small (Replay-Static +0.08, p=0.21) while the process term dominates (Active-Replay +0.26, p<1e-3).

  • Static ~ Recall < Active on shared cells
  • Total gain splits additively into content and process terms
  • Active still bundles retrieval control with multi-turn interaction and tool-use competence, which the authors do not separate

Scientific World Modeling: exploratory and unconfirmed

SWM adds a SIMULATE command sending the draft to a black-box world model returning novelty and feasibility on separate channels plus a next action. The strongest of five designs pools to +0.245, concentrated on small backbones — qwen-9b +0.61 (p=0.028) versus +0.14 and -0.06 on frontier backbones (p>0.6). Three controls undercut it.

  • Does not survive Holm multiple-comparison correction (p>=0.12)
  • Indistinguishable from a free-text aggregation variant (+0.03, p=0.75)
  • A compute-matched best-of-3 baseline meets or exceeds it
  • Frontier backbones appear to already run novelty/mechanism checks unprompted (illustrative, not causal)

Key findings

  • Active carries 4.4x the between-model score variance of Static and distinguishes 62% of top-half model pairs versus 12% for Static.
  • Ideation quality scales ~2.1x faster with model knowledge cutoff under Active (+1.16/year) than Static (+0.54/year); interaction +0.62/year, 95% CI [0.41,0.94], p<1e-3.
  • The gain is capability-gated: r=+0.69 (p<1e-4) with static ability; quartile means -0.18, +0.29, +0.49, +0.76; mean gain only +0.34.
  • The gain lands in grounding, not novelty: feasibility +1.21, clarity +0.63, specificity +0.58, impact +0.23, originality -0.14 (not significant).
  • Curated references barely beat closed-book recall (-0.09, p=0.11) while Active beats recall (+0.23, p<1e-3); passive replay of the agent's own references preserves only +0.08 (p=0.21) of the gap.
  • Scientific World Modeling gains +0.61 on qwen-9b (p=0.028) but +0.14 / -0.06 on frontier backbones; the pooled +0.245 fails Holm correction and a compute-matched best-of-3 baseline.

Notable quotes

The critic receives this evidence block, must justify its originality score against it, and is explicitly forbidden from overruling the retrieved evidence with its own memory.
We grade a single free-text paragraph rather than a filled-in template, because structured multi-slot forms reward exhaustive box-filling and depress originality, whereas a paragraph forces the model to commit to one idea.
We read the pattern as grounding without added novelty.
Reference presence alone adds little, and the gain requires the model to gather its own evidence.

Limitations

  • Every dimension score is an LLM judgment; originality is the weakest dimension for human agreement, and the authors call an expert novelty study the most important validation they did not run.
  • Active bundles retrieval control with multi-turn interaction and tool-use competence; no condition supplied agent-selected references without agent-driven turns.
  • The Scientific World Modeling result clears neither Holm correction nor a compute-matched baseline, and only four backbones were tested.

Open questions

  • Would domain experts rating novelty directly reproduce the flat-originality finding, or is it an artifact of the literature-verified critic?
  • Can retrieval control be isolated from multi-turn interaction and tool competence?
  • Would a differently designed generation-time loop help frontier backbones, and does such a loop flatter rather than stress-test the drafts it is given?
