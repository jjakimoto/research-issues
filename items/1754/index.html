    AlphaDiverse: Post-Training Local Quantitative Research Agents for Diverse Exploration in Alpha Factor Mining

    ← Go backWhere Does Exactly-Once Live? Model, Harness, and…NextThe authors' explanation of their own closed-book result… →
    📰 Latest · 2026-09-25 Full Article
    Read full article →View on GitHub

    Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
    Date: 2026-09-25

    Source: arxiv · 2026-09-24
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29014

    AlphaDiverse turns 72 API-generated research loops into local Qwen3.8-27B Planner and Realizer agents, then jointly optimizes predictive quality and complementary exploration. Across four Chinese equity universes it increases retained economic mechanisms and leads several risk-adjusted metrics, although evidence comes from one agentic run per method and market, gross of trading costs.

    Executive summary

    AlphaDiverse is a six-module alpha-mining system whose Planner proposes complementary economic mechanisms and whose Realizer converts each plan into two structurally different executable factors. Separate rank-64 LoRA adapters on a frozen Qwen3.8-27B backbone are initialized from selected API traces and jointly refined with GRPO using a utility weighted 75% toward predictive improvement and 25% toward incremental diversity. In January–August 2026 outer tests across four Chinese stock universes, the local system expanded useful-mechanism coverage and delivered strong risk-adjusted results, though the study excludes trading costs and lacks repeated agentic runs.

    Research loop and factor controls

    The workflow contains planning, capability retrieval, realization, execution, validation, and analysis. In each of 20 rounds, the Planner emits four plans based on distinct mechanisms spanning at least three economic themes; the Realizer emits two computationally distinct specifications per plan, yielding 160 candidate slots per run.

    • The mechanism catalogue contains 38 entries across momentum, reversal, liquidity, intraday structure, volatility, and crowding themes.
    • Execution rejects illegal operators, unsupported inputs, parameter violations, and look-ahead leakage.
    • Candidates must pass coverage, predictive-quality, and redundancy gates before LightGBM tests whether the accepted block improves the inner score.
    • The Analysis module summarizes evidence and retention decisions into the next-round state.

    Trace selection and local post-training

    Training data come from 72 API research loops spanning GPT-5.5, Grok-4.6, and GLM-5.3, four markets, multiple feature-mask rates, and one- versus three-fold inner evaluation. The final SFT corpus contains 589 Planner examples and 1,006 Realizer examples.

    • Planner examples target a 50/30/20 mixture of model-improvement, productive-switch, and new-direction traces.
    • Planner and Realizer use separate rank-64 LoRA adapters with scaling 128 on a frozen Qwen3.8-27B backbone.
    • Joint GRPO uses 96 fixed task states, producing 768 Planner responses and 1,220 Realizer responses.

    Joint reward and credit assignment

    A LightGBM evaluator scores accepted factors on a later inner reward window. Shared utility combines normalized predictive-score gain with a log-determinant reward for adding quality-weighted prediction-change directions beyond archived research history.

    • Utility is 0.75 predictive quality plus 0.25 incremental diversity.
    • Planner advantages compare portfolios of plans; Realizer advantages compare implementations conditioned on the same plans.
    • Invalid Realizer responses receive utility -1; invalid Planner responses receive -1 and suppress downstream generation.
    • Infrastructure failures cause the whole 4-by-2 sampling group to be skipped for both policy updates.

    Evaluation design and financial results

    Experiments use daily and minute-level data for CSI300, CSI500, CSI1000, and the broader Shanghai/Shenzhen A-share universe. Research and selection are confined to May 2022–December 2025; factors and model settings are frozen before evaluation on January–August 2026 open-t+1 to open-t+2 excess returns.

    • On CSI300, AlphaDiverse reached RIC 0.0407, annualized excess return 42.10%, information ratio 3.210, maximum drawdown 5.51%, and Calmar ratio 7.641.
    • Across the four markets, useful retained mechanisms were 23, 25, 29, and 28, versus 4, 6, 18, and 16 for SFT-only agents.
    • In a fixed-state transfer test, held-out useful-mechanism coverage was 12.6 on CSI300 versus 5.9 for GPT-5.5 and 2.3 for SFT-only.

    Deployment economics

    A complete 20-round CSI300 run used no external API calls for its local Planner, Realizer, or Analysis nodes. It consumed 1.436 allocated H200 GPU-hours, compared with estimated API charges of $7.28 for GPT-5.5, $5.57 for Grok-4.6, and $2.19 for GLM-5.3; these figures exclude trace collection and post-training.

    • The local run executed all 160 candidates, accepted 118, retained 56, and produced 150 distinct formulas.
    • Training used eight H200 GPUs, but total training duration, energy, and amortized trace-collection cost are not reported.

    Key findings

    • Joint post-training matters more than SFT alone: complete AlphaDiverse reached RIC 0.0407, annualized excess return 42.10%, and 23 useful mechanisms on CSI300.
    • Adding the diversity reward raised useful mechanisms from 18 to 23, reduced pair correlation from 0.365 to 0.316, and raised annualized excess return from 37.77% to 42.10%.
    • AlphaDiverse achieved the highest useful-mechanism coverage in all four markets.
    • Local deployment replaced per-run API fees with 1.436 H200 GPU-hours and retained 56 CSI300 factors, compared with 35 for GPT-5.5 and 8 for SFT-only.

    Limitations

    • Each agentic method–market configuration has only one complete mining run, so headline comparisons lack across-run uncertainty.
    • Returns are gross of trading costs, and evaluation covers four related Chinese equity universes over only eight months.
    • The fixed mechanism catalogue and operator library constrain exploration; total training time, energy, and trace-collection cost are not reported.

    Open questions

    • Do the gains persist across repeated mining seeds, longer regimes, other asset classes, and realistic turnover, slippage, and capacity costs?
    • Can the mechanism catalogue and operator language expand automatically while preserving causality and data-timing checks?
    • Can longer-horizon credit assignment better measure delayed factor contributions across evolving research states?
