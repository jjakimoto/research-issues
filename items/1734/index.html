    Propose, Don't Judge: An Anytime-Valid Referee for LLM Agents That Mine Investment Factors

    Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
    Date: 2026-09-24

    Source: arxiv · 2026-09-22
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27051

    Separating adaptive factor proposal from a frozen anytime-valid referee sharply reduces false admissions, but certification takes roughly 500 trading days and judging daily rank-IC can favor factors with poor trading economics. The result is stronger evidence for statistical governance than for superior portfolio returns.

    Executive summary

    The paper proposes governed self-evolution: an LLM may propose factors, diagnose retirements, and author probes, while a frozen statistical procedure alone admits and retires factors. Its referee bets on post-submission, predictably whitened daily rank-IC, applies online e-BH for anytime false-discovery control, and uses an e-detector for retirement. Across synthetic, diagnostic-probe, and CSI 500 walk-forward experiments, the referee limits false admissions, but its delay and one-day target reduce portfolio performance.

    Division of labor

    The controller can allocate submissions, diagnose alarms, and create sandboxed probes, but cannot modify the referee, factor library, point-in-time data layer, append-only decision log, or cost meter. Portfolio execution is downstream and does not feed back into the controller or referee.

    Anytime-valid referee

    Candidates are scored only on post-submission daily rank-IC. A predictably sized betting process supplies an e-value; online e-BH admits candidates across 2,000 frozen slots at alpha 0.05, while a restarted e-detector retires factors whose edge falls below 0.015.

    Evaluation design

    The factorial crosses round-robin, discounted-UCB, and LLM controllers with the frozen referee and three deliberately invalid alternatives: daily peeking, an age-relaxed threshold, and no gate. Evaluation covers planted-truth streams, 100-episode probe tests, and point-in-time CSI 500 data from January 2015 through August 2026.

    Governance and execution results

    The frozen referee reports zero false admissions per submission in the synthetic environment. On the four-family real-data library, round-robin produces 11.7 false admissions per campaign versus 86.2–196.0 under leaky referees. Certification delays true-factor admission by about 500 trading days, and certified portfolios trail ungated portfolios in net Sharpe.

    Key findings

    • With planted truth, the frozen referee produced 0.000 false admissions per submission for every controller, while leaky referees produced roughly 0.26–0.85.
    • Under the frozen referee, LLM realized yield exceeded round-robin in every start year across all six model-family and library settings, but comparisons with discounted UCB were mixed.
    • Authored probes significantly reduced intervention regret for DeepSeek, Muse, and Gemini after Holm correction; results for GPT, Hunyuan, and GLM were indistinguishable from zero.
    • The certified four-family book's net Sharpe was 0.33–0.50 versus 0.59–0.77 without a gate.
    • Momentum's one-day rank-IC is near 0.001 but reaches 0.009 at 63 days, exposing a mismatch between the certified statistic and traded horizon.

    Notable quotes

    The agent may propose. A statistical procedure that the agent cannot touch must judge.
    The guarantee holds regardless of who proposes.
    The proposer matters only when the referee leaks.
    A slow certificate is cheap when the edge persists and expensive when it decays.

    Limitations

    • The empirical sample covers one market and overlapping start years; borrow fees are omitted, and the portfolio results do not establish investability.
    • Historical replay may expose LLMs to information from the evaluated period, violating the no-foreknowledge condition.
    • The real-data label is a proxy overlapping the admission window, while the proof controls the whitened stream rather than the economic viability threshold.

    Open questions

    • Can an anytime-valid referee certify the return horizon actually traded without sacrificing its stopping-time guarantee?
    • Do the governance results replicate in another market and a genuinely held-out future period?
    • Can lower-noise statistics or structured hypothesis allocation shorten the roughly 500-day admission delay without weakening false-discovery control?
