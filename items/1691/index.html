    WeVisDoc: From Coverage to Capability for Robust End-to-End Document Parsing

    ← Go backHacker News — Front Page: New GPT-6 and Claude Opus 5.5…NextAutoGym: Blueprint-First Generation of Verifiable Agent Gyms →
    📰 Latest · 2026-09-22
    Read full article →View on GitHub

    Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
    Date: 2026-09-22

    Source: huggingface-daily-papers · 2026-09-17
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20423

    Tencent WeChat Vision shows that a purely data-centric recipe on a stock Qwen3-VL backbone — broad-coverage construction, then residual-diagnosed targeted data — beats every compared end-to-end document parser, with a 4B model hitting 95.38 Overall on OmniDocBench v1.6 and the biggest wins on degraded, photographed pages. Weights for both 2B and 4B are on Hugging Face.

    Executive summary

    WeVisDoc is a two-stage, data-centric framework for end-to-end document parsing built on unmodified Qwen3-VL-Instruct 2B/4B backbones - no detector, OCR engine, table parser or custom decoder. Stage I trains on ~40M records spanning heterogeneous supervision granularities, synthesized executable page programs and structure-preserving appearance degradations, budgeted in supervised target tokens rather than record counts. Stage II uses a held-out diagnostic probe to measure residual error inside fixed visual-structural clusters, distinguishing missing data support from insufficient exposure, then reallocates a fixed token budget over a ~5M-record refinement pool. WeVisDoc-4B reaches 95.38 Overall on OmniDocBench v1.6 and 75.54 Avg3 on PureDocBench.

    Architecture: deliberately vanilla

    The claim is that gains come from data, not architecture. Both scales keep the native dynamic-resolution ViT, the autoregressive LLM, tokenizer and positional encoding of Qwen3-VL-Instruct. Output is a single shared serialization: reading-order Markdown, HTML tables, LaTeX formulas.

    • Backbones: Qwen3-VL-Instruct 2B and 4B
    • No external document detector, OCR engine, table parser or specialized decoder
    • Stage I trains ViT + LLM; Stage II freezes the ViT and refines only the LLM
    • Fused AdamW, lr 5e-6 cosine with 500-step warmup, weight decay 0.1, global batch 512, sequence length 8,192, bf16 + FlashAttention + DeepSpeed ZeRO-2

    Stage I - broad coverage, budgeted in tokens not records

    About 40M records span page-, region- and component-level supervision across layouts, languages and acquisition conditions, combining converted source labels, multi-model joint annotation with difficulty control, compositional synthetic document programs dual-compiled from semantic HTML, and source-conditioned appearance degradation.

    • Optimization budget is defined over loss-bearing target tokens, since record target lengths vary widely
    • Source share omega_s = M_s^rho / sum M_s'^rho damps domination by the largest corpora
    • A length-correction step converts desired token shares into record-sampling probabilities
    • Appearance variants expand visual coverage but are not counted as additional semantic support

    Stage II - capability diagnosis and residual-driven refinement

    A probe set disjoint from training, mining and final evaluation measures residual errors inside fixed visual-structural clusters. The central distinction is between weaknesses from absent empirical support and weaknesses from low exposure to existing support.

    • ~5M-record refinement pool: verified hard examples, targeted real and synthetic additions, plus high-quality Stage I replay
    • Document-level and component-level hard-example mining with a high-error audit
    • Targeted page programs synthesized for difficult element families, validated back against their cluster
    • Hard-example stream separately capped; all exposure charged against a fixed Stage II target-token budget

    Evaluation protocol

    OmniDocBench v1.6 (1,651 pages) tests coverage under naturally occurring diversity; PureDocBench tests paired degradation robustness against source-derived ground truth. Every number is the mean of three inference runs with deterministic decoding and no reranking.

    • PureDocBench: 1,475 HTML/CSS source pages, 10 domains, 66 subcategories, rendered into Clean / Digital Degraded / Real Degraded views sharing one ground truth
    • Ten synthetic degradation scenarios and four physical or screen-mediated acquisition pipelines
    • Avg3 = mean of the three PureDocBench track Overall scores
    • Motivation: an audit found 2,580 confirmed annotation errors among 21,353 evaluator-scored blocks in OmniDocBench v1.5

    Release

    Project page, code and both checkpoints are public. The training data pools are described but not announced as a release.

    • Weights: WeVisDoc-2B and WeVisDoc-4B on Hugging Face under the Tencent org
    • Code: github.com/Tencent/WeVisDoc; project page tencent.github.io/WeVisDoc
    • Authors: WeChat Vision, Tencent Inc.

    Key findings

    • WeVisDoc-4B scores 95.38 Overall on OmniDocBench v1.6, beating the best compared end-to-end specialist (HunyuanOCR-1.5, 94.74) by 0.64 points, and also leads that series on TextEdit (0.036), FormulaCDM (96.81), TableTEDS_S (95.34) and ROEdit (0.125).
    • On PureDocBench, WeVisDoc-4B posts the highest Avg3 at 75.54, exceeding the leading broad-domain baseline by 1.43 points, with 79.81 Clean, 77.74 Digital Degraded and 69.08 Real Degraded.
    • The 2B model reaches 95.06 OmniDocBench Overall and 73.86 Avg3 - top-tier end-to-end quality at half the 4B parameter count.
    • Stage II is worth +1.56 OmniDocBench / +1.74 Avg3 at 2B and +1.16 / +2.35 at 4B, concentrated on degradation: +4.03 on Real Degraded at 4B versus only +0.49 on Clean.
    • Data scale: ~40M records in the Stage I pool and ~5M in the Stage II refinement pool, budgeted in loss-bearing target tokens.
    • Scaling 2B to 4B helps most where appearance shift is strongest: +0.45 Clean, +1.12 Digital Degraded, +3.48 Real Degraded.

    Notable quotes

    Both scales retain the native dynamic-resolution vision transformer (ViT), autoregressive large language model (LLM), tokenizer, and positional encoding, and neither introduces an external document detector, OCR engine, table parser, or specialized decoder.
    WeVisDoc-4B establishes a new state of the art among the compared end-to-end specialists, achieving 95.38 Overall and improving the previous leading result by 0.64 points.
    Scaling from 2B to 4B yields gains of 0.45, 1.12, and 3.48 points on Clean, Digital Degraded, and Real Degraded, respectively, indicating that additional capacity is most beneficial under stronger appearance shifts.
    PureDocBench comprises 1,475 HTML/CSS source pages from 10 domains and 66 subcategories.

    Limitations

    • The Stage I vs Stage II comparison is a whole-stage ablation, not component-wise, so the contribution of residual-aware allocation itself is not isolated.
    • Evidence is bounded by benchmark coverage and by the fidelity of synthetic degradations; handwriting, historical scans and severely damaged documents remain insufficiently measured.
    • PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 (0.9B) still outscores WeVisDoc-4B on OmniDocBench Overall at 96.33, so the SOTA claim is scoped to compared end-to-end specialists.

    Open questions

    • Does residual-aware token allocation carry its own measurable gain once continued optimization, hard examples and replay are held constant?
    • Would the same coverage-then-capability recipe transfer to pipeline-based parsers or to larger backbones?
    • How well do structure-preserving synthetic degradations stand in for authentic acquisition noise on handwriting and historical scans?
