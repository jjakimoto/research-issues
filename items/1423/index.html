Nyström Attention Matches Full Attention for Cross-Sectional Stock Prediction

📰 Latest · 2026-09-10 Full Article
Read full article →

Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
Date: 2026-09-10

Source: arxiv · 2026-09-08
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08106

Decomposing MASTER's inter-stock attention shows its value lies in a near-uniform but low-rank deviation (effective rank ~65, top-10 modes = 96.5% of energy), so Nyström attention with 32 landmarks is TOST-certified equivalent to full O(N^2) attention at CSI300 and CSI800 while every sparsification variant degrades. The gains do not transfer: at N≈3,500 no cross-stock module beats a per-stock LSTM.

Executive summary

The paper audits Step 3 of MASTER (inter-stock multi-head attention, 329,216 params = 42.5% of the model, −25.3% IC when removed) with diagnostics, ablations, SVD and efficient-attention experiments. Attention is near-uniform (entropy 5.63 vs ln300=5.70, perplexity 278/300), yet the 1.3%-energy deviation from uniformity is the sole source of cross-sectional variance and is low-rank — a property already present in randomly initialized models. Nyström (m=32) matches full attention while top-K, graph masking and other sparse variants fail. Efficiency crosses over at N*≈1,300, reaching 14.7x latency and 11.2x memory savings at N=9,000, but at N≈3,500 no cross-stock module beats PureLSTM.

What the module is worth

A five-stage pipeline ablation on CSI300 (seed 0) isolates Step 3 as the dominant contributor and the only path for cross-stock information flow. Removing Step 4 (temporal aggregation) actually raises Rank IC.

  • Full MASTER IC 0.0646 / Rank IC 0.0685; no Step 3 IC 0.0482 (−25.3%)
  • Step 3 holds 329,216 parameters, 42.5% of the model
  • Dropping Step 4 raises Rank IC to 0.0722 (+5.4%)

Attention diagnostics: near-uniform and complementarity-seeking

Extracted over 619 test days, per-row entropy is within 1.2% of the uniform maximum and the time-averaged matrix sorted by Shenwan L1 industry shows no block structure. Attention anti-correlates with return similarity and reorganizes on a 2-4 week horizon.

  • Spearman rho = −0.614 between pairwise attention and pairwise return correlation (n = 73,910 pairs)
  • −0.645 unconditional on the 423 industry-labeled stocks; partial rho = −0.627 after controlling for same-industry, beta and volatility difference
  • Negative on all 619 test days (mean −0.215, std 0.071)
  • Centered cosine similarity decays 0.967 (lag-1) to 0.048 (lag-20); PC1 of daily deviations explains only 9% of variance

Why sparsification fails

Every graph-guided or sparse variant degrades; hard masking to correlation neighbors is worse than deleting the module entirely. When most weights are ~1/N, the top-K entries are themselves near-equal (perplexity 15.9/16 at K=16), so subtracting a constant cannot create discrimination — IC needs breadth of aggregation while Rank IC needs weight sharpening.

  • GraphMask+Corr IC 0.045 ± 0.004 vs No-Step-3 floor 0.050 ± 0.001 (4 seeds)
  • Original vs GraphMask paired t p = 0.040
  • TopK K=16 retains at most 50% of either metric
  • Deviation is only 1.3% of output energy but carries all cross-sectional variance

Spectral structure and Nyström

The SVD of D = A − (1/N)11^T has effective rank ~65 of 300, top-10 singular values capturing 96.5% of the Frobenius norm; median effective rank across 208 day-by-head matrices is 65 (IQR 45-81). The same structure exists in untrained models (rank 59 ± 7, 95.6 ± 0.9% energy), so low-rank is architectural. Nyström with m=32 landmarks resampled per forward pass (pseudo-inverse via 6 Newton-Schulz iterations) matches full attention with lower seed variance; a LearnedLowRank r=32 static control collapses to the floor.

  • Nyström m=32: IC 0.059 ± 0.002, Rank IC 0.066 ± 0.003 vs Original 0.058 ± 0.007 / 0.066 ± 0.003 (5 seeds)
  • TOST: Rank IC equivalent within ±0.005 (p=0.003); IC within ±0.008 (p=0.028) but not ±0.005 (p=0.099)
  • Landmark count is non-monotonic: peak at m=32, dip at m=48 (1.2 sigma), below peak at m=64-128
  • LearnedLowRank IC 0.0478 vs no-module 0.0482; StaticAttn oracle recovers only ~9% of IC value

Scale and efficiency

At CSI800 (10 seeds) equivalence is certified on both metrics within ±0.005, and m=80 does not beat m=32. At N≈3,500 on the full A-share market with an adapted architecture (17 OHLCV features, d_model=64, no gating) no cross-stock module beats a per-stock LSTM. Efficiency is measured on a Tesla T4 with 50 timed passes.

  • CSI800 TOST: IC p=0.038, Rank IC p=0.034 (m=32 = 4% coverage)
  • N≈3,500: PureLSTM IC 0.043 ± 0.002 / Sharpe 5.6 ± 0.5 vs MASTER+Nyst32 IC 0.037 ± 0.008 (paired t p=0.27)
  • Newton-Schulz adds a fixed ~4 ms overhead; latency crossover at N*≈1,300
  • Memory from N=300 to 9,000: full grows 219x, Nyström 20x; full OOMs at N=12,000 where Nyström uses 974 MB
  • Nyström training is ~2x slower per epoch

SVD components as an implicit factor model

Mapping the top components of the deviation matrix to financial characteristics shows six components (91% of energy) with interpretable meaning, dominated by a volatility/market mode. The paper reads the module as an implicit 6-factor risk model learned without factor labels.

  • PC1 = 79.5% of energy, volatility/market mode (vol rho +0.48)
  • PC4 gives the cleanest industry signal, eta^2 = 0.48 (defensive vs cyclical)
  • PCs 2,3,5,7 map to momentum, old-vs-new economy, liquidity, reversal
  • PCs 6 and 8-10 (3.5% energy) are not interpretable

Key findings

  • Step 3 inter-stock attention is 42.5% of MASTER's parameters and its removal costs −25.3% IC (0.0646 -> 0.0482).
  • Attention is near-uniform (entropy 5.63 nats vs ln300 = 5.70, perplexity 278/300) but the 1.3%-of-energy deviation is the sole carrier of cross-sectional variance.
  • The deviation matrix is low-rank (effective rank ~65 of 300, top-10 modes = 96.5% of energy) and holds in untrained models too, so low-rank is inherent to softmax attention.
  • Nyström m=32 is TOST-equivalent to full attention: Rank IC within ±0.005 (p=0.003) at CSI300 across 5 seeds, and within ±0.005 on both IC (p=0.038) and Rank IC (p=0.034) at CSI800 across 10 seeds.
  • Landmark count is non-monotonic — m=32 beats m=48 and m=64-128 — because over-sizing the landmark set worsens pseudo-inverse conditioning.
  • Sparse alternatives all fail: GraphMask+Corr IC 0.045 ± 0.004 is below the no-module floor of 0.050 ± 0.001.
  • Efficiency crosses over at N*≈1,300 and reaches 14.7x latency / 11.2x memory savings at N=9,000, with full attention OOM at N=12,000.
  • At N≈3,500 no cross-stock module beats PureLSTM (paired t p=0.27 on IC, p=0.53 on Rank IC, n=4).

Notable quotes

Thus, low-rank is inherent to softmax attention over N items; what training contributes is the specific pattern of deviations carrying predictive value.
A static low-rank attention, even when optimized end-to-end, collapses to the no-attention floor (IC = 0.0478 ≈ 0.0482).
Entropy is a bulk statistic that cannot distinguish every weight equaling 1/N from most weights near 1/N with a structured low-rank deviation.
This represents an honest boundary of our core claim: Nyström equivalence is robust under standard preprocessing but narrows under optimized preprocessing.

Limitations

  • Most CSI300 decomposition ablations rest on a single seed; only the Nyström equivalence claim is multi-seed.
  • The N≈3,500 experiments use an adapted pipeline, so they are not directly comparable to the CSI300 analysis.
  • One model in one market (Chinese A-shares), all Sharpe ratios frictionless, and the TOST Rank IC equivalence weakens from p=0.003 to p=0.120 under per-day cross-sectional z-scoring.

Open questions

  • Is the low-rank deviation structure a general property of cross-stock attention, or specific to MASTER and A-shares?
  • Can cross-stock mechanisms be designed to enhance cross-sectional differentiation rather than smooth it?
  • Does Nyström retain its advantage inside MASTER's full native pipeline at N > 3,000?
