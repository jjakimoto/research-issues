The Fundamental Structure of Risk: From Characteristics to Covariance

📰 Latest · 2026-07-30
Read full article →

Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; and LLMs & multimodal LLMs — methods, papers, tools
Date: 2026-07-30

Source: arXiv q-fin.ST (Alouadi & Lehalle) · 2026-07-27
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24410

Alouadi and Charles-Albert Lehalle introduce the Characteristic-Driven Dynamic Factor Model (CD-DFM), a non-linear latent factor model that builds the asset cross-section from observable firm fundamentals/characteristics rather than from noisy return histories, trained end-to-end on a Stein-covariance loss plus a factor-reconstruction term. Because the encoder depends only on characteristics, it can embed previously unseen assets at inference with no retraining ('zero-shot onboarding'), while jointly producing interpretable factor exposures and a forward covariance estimator. On S&P 500 equities it yields economically structured latent representations and covariance forecasts the authors call competitive using lower-frequency inputs than return-based methods. Caveat: the abstract reports no hard error/benchmark numbers, so 'competitive' is unquantified; relevance to portfolio construction and risk is high (Lehalle co-author).
