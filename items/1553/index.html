Discovery Foundation Models: Toward Open-Ended Discovery Intelligence

📰 Latest · 2026-09-16 Full Article
Read full article →

Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
Date: 2026-09-16

Source: huggingface-daily-papers · 2026-09-15
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15973

A position-plus-architecture paper that reframes the next frontier of foundation models as 'Discovery Intelligence' — systems that revise the research structure itself, not just answers inside it — pinned to a concrete topology (revisable research state + Research World Model gating + multi-criterion verifiers) and one real wet-lab loop where the verifier of last resort is a physical measurement, not an LLM judge.

Executive summary

Yang, Yin and Wu (arXiv:2609.15973v1 [cs.CL], 14 Sep 2026) define Discovery Foundation Models (DFMs) as model SYSTEMS, not checkpoints, characterized by seven coupled capabilities operating over a revisable research state. They instantiate this as Zetema, whose central constraint is that every scientifically consequential operation must leave an inspectable state transition: branches are first-class, a Research World Model predicts observations/costs/risks before acting, and a multi-criterion verifier gate (V_j(a) >= tau_j for all j) decides whether an action is eligible. Grounding is explicitly tiered — Dry-Lab outputs are predictions and never enter the evidence state with the same epistemic status as measurements. The empirical case, GALILEO, is lab-validated, with two peptide branches confirmed by patient-derived organoid IC50s of 18.922 uM and 0.736 uM. The paper reports no benchmark numbers of its own: training and evaluation protocols are formulated, not run.

The gap: optimizing inside a supplied structure vs. revising it

A conventional task is abstracted as Q = (P, R, G, T, V) — problem, representation, objective, tools, evaluator — all supplied. Discovery instead maps (W, K_0, B) -> (dK, Xi): externally validated knowledge progress plus an evidence-bearing record of state transitions, where the problem, representation, hypothesis space, intervention strategy AND validation procedure can all change mid-episode. The authors localize the gap to three missing transitions: Framing, Modeling, and Grounding-and-revision.

  • Agentic automation can pursue a misframed question more efficiently without getting better at recognizing the misframing
  • Valid outputs include negative results: that an effect does not replicate, or that a question is untestable under current measurements
  • Capability is defined behaviorally, not architecturally

System topology: Zetema's generator / gate / environment loop

Zetema is a functional tuple D = (pi_theta, S, M, T, E, V, H). Within a task, S_{t+1} = F(S_t, a_t, o_t), where the action space includes both ordinary tool calls and operations on the research program itself (split a problem, add a latent variable, change scale, replace a representation, construct an adversarial explanation, request replication, terminate a branch). The state is typed and linked, with a representation revision explicitly NOT stored as another hypothesis and a predicted outcome explicitly NOT stored as an observed measurement.

  • Branches are first-class objects — comparable, mergeable, archivable, restorable after new evidence
  • The 'reviser' is a typed Revise() operator with explicit failure attribution (theory failure vs. measurement error vs. protocol deviation vs. confounding vs. simulator misspecification)
  • Search is deliberately not the unit: the unit of computation is a transition in a revisable research state rather than a completed textual stage
  • Human edits, verifier rejections and permission boundaries are retained in the state because they affect attribution and the later training signal

Verifier grounding: a portfolio, not an LLM judge

Before an action acquires cost, a Research World Model screens counterfactual consequences — it explicitly does not certify truth. Eligibility is a conjunction of per-criterion thresholds: a is eligible iff V_j(a) >= tau_j for all j in J, where each V_j may be a structured verifier, an uncertainty-aware model, a formal constraint, or a HUMAN decision. Thresholds are action-scaled: speculative hypotheses may enter under uncertainty, while costly or irreversible interventions require stronger evidence and permission.

  • A formal claim can go straight to proof checking; a physical intervention can require structural checks, provenance review, power analysis, simulation, adversarial falsification, feasibility checks and human authorization
  • Validation is deliberately NOT collapsed to a scalar reward — the system must preserve which validator supported which state transition
  • World-model error is treated as a selection effect; high uncertainty triggers a bounded pilot, not automatic rejection
  • Digital-only loops are called out as self-confirming when one model writes the code, picks the metric, makes the test and reads the result

GALILEO: the lab-validated grounding case

GALILEO (Jiang et al., 2026) is a therapeutic-peptide system coupling a cognitive layer to robotic synthesis, QC, liquid handling, imaging and plate-based phenotyping. Two externally grounded branches are reported: GALILEO-LRC, supported by selective LRRC8A/LRRC8C current blockade in whole-cell electrophysiology plus patient-derived organoid activity at IC50 = 18.922 uM; and GALILEO-SLC, supported by mitochondrial localization plus SLC25A1-high organoid dose-response at IC50 = 0.736 uM. Across five autonomous optimization rounds the search was non-monotonic — rounds 2-3 pruned weakly supported branches, round 4 REOPENED previously abandoned motif organizations, round 5 consolidated them — yielding the transferable Amphiphilic Balance Grammar.

  • Mechanism hypotheses were themselves revised by evidence
  • Not all steps are robotic: electrophysiology, metabolic profiling and in-vivo validation can remain agent-specified but human-executed, and must stay in the provenance record
  • The authors explicitly disclaim generality — GALILEO's domain and objectives remain substantially structured

Capability formation: seven training signals, no reported runs

Trainable parts are a policy, state updater, world model, process verifier and skill selector. The composite objective L_DFM sums stage-weighted action imitation, state-transition modeling, state-matched pairwise preference, multi-criterion verifier BCE, world-model NLL, KL-regularized RL from scientific feedback, and contrastive validated-skill retrieval. The per-step scientific reward combines knowledge gain, information gain, revision and validation quality against cost and risk penalties, plus a terminal transfer bonus.

  • Success-only trajectories are declared insufficient: malformed questions, non-identifying interventions, failed replications and abandoned branches must be retained with their SOURCE visible
  • Discovery Skills are stored as (trigger, operation, expected effect, required validating evidence) and promoted in stages: archived-trajectory replay -> shadow mode -> active influence; versioned and reversible
  • Process-level scaling splits the budget across phases, with falsification given an explicit budget so generation and confirmation do not monopolize compute

Process-centered evaluation and governance

Discovery Progress is reported as two separate axes — externally validated progress now, and improved future discovery behavior. Efficiency is dK_val / C_total with compute, money, expert time, physical experiments and risk reported separately because they are not interchangeable. Transfer is measured against a control ladder (no memory / fact-only / full-trajectory retrieval / successful-solution retrieval / domain skill memory / Discovery Skill Memory) and reported as a curve over episodes. Section 9 adds recursive-update risk: audit records, rollback, permission boundaries and independent evaluation channels should be insulated from ordinary self-modification.

  • Diagnostic cost metrics: cost to first identifying intervention, repeated non-identifying actions, experiments needed to eliminate a wrong mechanism, regret vs. an expert policy
  • Benchmarks should withhold or corrupt the structures the system must construct and split on mechanisms rather than NL topics
  • Independence is treated as a spectrum — two reviewers can share a base model, two simulations the same assumptions
  • Discriminative predictions should be committed before results arrive

Key findings

  • The DFM target is factored into exactly seven coupled capabilities (find, form, repr, hyp, int, rev, cont); the first six operate within an investigation and the seventh is the cross-task update mechanism.
  • Verifier grounding is explicitly a portfolio with per-criterion thresholds — a is eligible iff V_j(a) >= tau_j for all j — where V_j may be a structured verifier, an uncertainty-aware model, a formal constraint, or a human decision; validation is never collapsed to one scalar reward.
  • Epistemic tiering is hard-coded into the state: simulated observations do not enter the evidence state with the same status as measurements; the Research World Model screens consequences but does not certify truth.
  • The only quantitative results are lab-validated, not benchmark: GALILEO-LRC reaches IC50 = 18.922 uM and GALILEO-SLC 0.736 uM (~26x more potent) in patient-derived organoids, each backed by an orthogonal assay.
  • Across five wet-lab optimization rounds the design policy was non-monotonic — round 4 reopened motif organizations abandoned in rounds 2-3 — and the feedback was distilled into the reusable Amphiphilic Balance Grammar.
  • Training is specified as a seven-term composite loss plus a six-term per-step scientific reward with explicit cost and risk penalties; no training run, ablation, or benchmark score is reported anywhere in the paper.
  • Cost/efficiency is elevated to a first-class metric with compute, money, human time and risk reported separately and baselines required to be resource-matched.

Notable quotes

A fixed representation cannot express a variable it omits.
Zetema is built around one constraint: a scientifically consequential operation must leave an inspectable state transition.
Validation is not reduced to a universal scalar reward.
The verifier is not a replacement for external evidence.

Limitations

  • This is a framework/position paper: Zetema, the training objective and the evaluation protocol are formulated but never instantiated or measured — no benchmark results, ablations, baselines, or reliability numbers for the proposed system itself.
  • The single empirical grounding, GALILEO, is a prior third-party system in one structured domain; the authors concede its problem domain and objectives remain substantially structured and that cross-domain transfer is unsolved.
  • Key quantities are conceptual rather than operational — the intervention objective, reward weights, and verifier thresholds tau_j are unspecified, so the gate's actual cost / false-rejection behavior is unknown.

Open questions

  • How do you actually measure improvement in future discovery behavior while controlling for near-duplicate retrieval, prompt reuse, extra domain facts and extra compute?
  • Can a Research World Model be calibrated well enough to gate expensive physical interventions without its selection effect quietly narrowing the hypothesis space?
  • What validation regime makes recursive updates to evaluators, benchmark generators and memory policies safe, given the warning that measured performance can improve while genuine discovery capability narrows?
