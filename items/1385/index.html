What survives honest evaluation? Leakage-safe, search-aware assessment of LLM-driven trading strategy discovery

📰 Latest · 2026-09-08 Full Article
Read full article →

Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
Date: 2026-09-08

Source: arxiv · 2026-08-27
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27734

When an LLM strategy-discovery agent is forced to record every one of its own backtests and deflate by that trial count, none of its discoveries certify — across two frontier models, 100-candidate budgets and five repeated runs — while passive buy-and-hold does; and a planted look-ahead oracle at Sharpe 35 sails through Deflated Sharpe and PBO untouched. The clearest demonstration yet that leakage-safety and multiple-testing correction are complementary, not substitutes, for any self-reporting agentic search.

Executive summary

Gencay builds an LLM trading-strategy discovery system in which the two standard corrections — leakage-safety and search-intensity deflation — are structural rather than promised: the agent can only act through registry-validated tools whose feature registry excludes look-ahead constructions, and every strategy evaluation the search performs is written to a trial ledger that becomes the deflator. A six-experiment protocol (E1-E6b) on a 453-stock point-in-time US equity universe and a 39-ETF multi-asset universe, with commissions, spreads, square-root impact and borrow costs, then asks what survives. The answer is symmetric: passive premia are certified, every LLM-discovered strategy is rejected — including ones genuinely profitable out of sample — and a planted leaky oracle posting Sharpe 34.7 in-sample / 51.5 out-of-sample gets DSR = 1.00, proving deflation cannot catch contamination.

The architecture: honesty by construction

Strategies are declarative JSON plans — typed feature specs, an optional signal graph, a weighted combination, and one of five portfolio archetypes. The agent never writes code; it composes plans by calling registry-generated tools, each validated in-loop against parameter schemas, with invalid calls returning structured errors the model self-corrects. Lags accept non-negative shift only, and look-ahead features are simply absent from the agent-selectable registry.

  • Validated action surface doubles as a safety boundary: look-ahead is inexpressible, not merely discouraged
  • A deterministic audit layer independently enforces publication delays for external event data
  • Every plan is human-readable at the feature level — no generated code to audit
  • Ablation: a JSON-emitting critic without in-loop validation produced 0 of 60 usable candidates

Search-aware evaluation: four instruments plus an evaporation curve

Each reported arm gets the Deflated Sharpe Ratio computed against the search's own recorded trial count and Sharpe dispersion, the probability of backtest overfitting via combinatorially symmetric cross-validation, stationary-bootstrap confidence intervals, and paired tests against buy-and-hold. The evaporation curve plots the running best in-sample Sharpe against the rising deflation threshold implied by the growing ledger. Pre-registered hypotheses enter at N=1, so their deflated verdict collapses to their PSR.

  • Deflation threshold grows with the inverse normal of (1 - 1/N): a 10,000-candidate search faces a bar roughly 1.5x higher than a 100-candidate one at equal dispersion
  • Certification level used throughout is 0.95
  • 'Certified' is defined as an evaluation-window Sharpe CI excluding zero

Experimental design: data, windows, costs

E1-E4 use daily OHLCV for 453 liquid US large-cap stocks plus SPY (Tiingo, 2015-2026), with the tradeable set re-selected point-in-time as the top 200 names by trailing 63-day dollar volume every 21 trading days. E5-E6b use 39 multi-asset ETFs (2005-2026). Design/eval splits are 2017-2021 / 2022-2025 and 2007-2016 / 2017-2025. Costs are 1 bp commission, 2 bp spread, square-root impact against trailing 21-day dollar volume, and 50 bp annualized borrow on short notional.

  • Agent runs use OpenAI gpt-4.1 with cross-model replication on Anthropic claude-sonnet-5
  • The deterministic experiments involve no LLM calls and reproduce exactly
  • The nine-year window is a deliberate power choice: t grows as SR times sqrt(years)

Results: rejection of the agents, certification of the passive arms

E1's leaky oracle posts design Sharpe 34.7 and evaluation Sharpe 51.5 with DSR = 1.00. E2's classic factors all sit at DSR <= 0.32 with search PBO = 0.83, while equal-weight buy-and-hold clears at 0.97. E3's 100-candidate autonomous search produces an RSI x volume contrarian book at design Sharpe 1.69 that stops at DSR 0.86 against a threshold of 1.21, then collapses to evaluation Sharpe 0.18. E5 moves to the fair field where the referee does certify SPY and the 39-ETF buy-and-hold, and where PBO = 0.80 predicted the agent's out-of-sample degradation.

  • Three failure modes, three instruments: selection luck (DSR), predicted rank degradation (CSCV/PBO), out-of-sample collapse (held-out CI)
  • In E3 PBO was low (0.01) — the winner kept its rank while losing its absolute edge, so only the held-out interval caught it
  • claude-sonnet-5 explored more diversely (39 distinct feature combinations vs gpt-4.1's 22) and still failed: DSR 0.18, PBO 0.51

The human production system and the robustness of the null

E4 stress-tests costs at 0.5x and 2x and narrows/widens the universe; the null holds everywhere, and five independent repetitions all converge on the same volatility-breakout family, all five profitable out of sample, none certifiable. E6b ports the author's own weekly PineScript rule engine into an 84-node signal graph, cross-validated bar-for-bar with zero mismatching bars on all four entry/exit conditions before any performance was examined.

  • Production system: design +34% (PSR 0.85) but evaluation Sharpe -0.12 / -13% against gold buy-and-hold's +257%
  • Paired bootstrap against holding gold rejects the production system at p < 0.001
  • The author's own trading-platform backtest independently corroborates buy-and-hold finishing ahead

Generalization beyond finance

The paper argues the pattern is domain-independent: any agentic system that searches a solution space and self-reports its best result inflates its trial count, so honest reporting requires the search to be recorded and paid for. Trading is merely the domain where the accounting instruments already exist.

  • The design pattern offered is: validated tool surface + complete trial ledger + deflation indexed to it
  • For a code agent: sequestered-test-set performance judged against the number of candidate patches scored on the visible tests
  • The authors explicitly note they have not tested the pattern outside finance

Key findings

  • Deflation cannot catch leakage: a deliberately leaky oracle posts design Sharpe 34.7 and evaluation Sharpe 51.5 and receives DSR = 1.00, passing every statistical test.
  • In E3 the agent composed 100 strategies through the validated tool loop (99/100 valid plans, ledger N=102); its best find reached design Sharpe 1.69 while its own deflation threshold chased it to 1.21, leaving DSR at 0.86, and the held-out window collapsed it to Sharpe 0.18 (+4.7%) versus buy-and-hold's +40.8%.
  • Cross-model replication on claude-sonnet-5 reached the same verdict via a more diverse search: 80/100 valid proposals, 39 distinct feature combinations, best find degrading to -0.33 (-29%) out of sample, DSR = 0.18, PBO = 0.51.
  • The validated action surface is what makes the plans usable at all: 99 of 100 candidates were valid interpretable plans, versus 0 of 60 for a JSON-emitting critic without in-loop validation.
  • The referee does say yes: SPY (eval Sharpe 0.85, +249%, CI [+0.24, +1.53]) and equal-weight multi-asset buy-and-hold (0.71, +108%, CI [+0.07, +1.46]) are certified, while time-series momentum returning +55% over nine held-out years is still under-powered at t about 1.5.
  • Raw out-of-sample profit is not evidence: the random-weights null returned +23% over nine evaluation years with design Sharpe -0.57 and DSR = 0.0008; five independent repetitions all converged on the volatility-breakout family, all profitable out of sample (eval SR 0.59-0.70), with DSR 0.15-0.29 and 0/5 surviving.
  • The ported human production system (84-node signal graph, zero mismatching bars against the original PineScript) was profitable in development (+34%, PSR 0.85) but returned -13% over 2017-2025 against gold buy-and-hold's +257%, rejected at p < 0.001.

Notable quotes

Deflation corrects for selection among honestly computed backtests; it has no mechanism against a strategy whose information set is contaminated, and the contamination survives out of sample by construction.
Look-ahead is not discouraged; it is inexpressible.
The instruments are not a universal rejector; they pass real premia and reject noise, both behaviors on the same table.
Non-certification is not non-existence; the referee reports evidence, not conviction, and quantifies exactly how much evidence a claimed edge still lacks.

Limitations

  • Survivorship: the equity constituent list is a fixed current list with only eight mid-sample delistings, which flatters active strategies and therefore biases against the paper's own null result.
  • News-sentiment and point-in-time fundamental features were excluded from the headline runs, and only gpt-4.1 and claude-sonnet-5 were tested — with three of five gpt-4.1 repetitions composing near-identical best strategies.
  • DSR assumes exchangeable trials while the hypothesis rotation makes candidates heterogeneous; PBO with about 20 candidates is coarse; and the 2017-2025 evaluation window contains a historic gold and equity bull that disadvantages timing systems.

Open questions

  • Does the pattern (validated tool surface + complete trial ledger + deflation indexed to it) transfer outside finance — e.g. FDR control over every hypothesis a scientific-discovery agent scored?
  • Can any LLM search budget produce a certifiable edge, given the deflation threshold grows with the inverse normal of (1 - 1/N)?
  • Would restoring the excluded feature classes, or moving to higher-frequency regimes where power accumulates in months rather than decades, change the verdict?
