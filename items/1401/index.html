AlphaRJM: Reward-Jump Memory for Stochastic Return-Guided Alpha Discovery

📰 Latest · 2026-09-09 Full Article
Read full article →

Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
Date: 2026-09-09

Source: arxiv · 2026-09-08
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08581

AlphaRJM adds an event-driven memory that only updates at terminal formula evaluations plus an action-conditioned neural-SDE critic to RL-based formulaic alpha mining, topping 10 of 12 dataset-metric cells on CSI300/500/800 — but the ICs are tiny (0.036-0.046), seed dispersion overlaps the margins, and the wealth curves ignore transaction costs.

Executive summary

Formulaic alpha discovery is a pool-dependent symbolic search where feedback arrives only when a complete expression is evaluated. AlphaRJM (IIT Guwahati) introduces Reward-Jump Memory — a latent state frozen during token construction that jumps only at terminal events using the realized pool reward and a categorical outcome in {accepted, replaced, rejected, invalid} — plus an action-conditioned SDE return critic whose Euler-Maruyama particles give a mean and an uncertainty for action scoring, trained with a CRPS/energy-distance distributional Bellman loss. On CSI300/500/800 (2010-2020 train, 2021 validation, 2022-2024 test, h=20, 4 seeds) it is best on 10 of 12 cells.

Problem framing

Following AlphaGen, a candidate formula is rewarded by its marginal contribution to the combined alpha ensemble, so usefulness depends on the pool already built. Conditioning only on the current pool throws away information: two trajectories can reach the same pool after different sequences of realized rewards and outcomes (a rejection is informative even though the pool is unchanged).

  • Reward is the marginal change in training-set ensemble IC against the pool.
  • Terminal outcome is categorical over {accepted, replaced, rejected, invalid}.
  • At an intermediate token step the consequence of an action is unobserved, so future discovery return is a distribution.

Reward-Jump Memory

A persistent latent state evolves by discrete jumps only at terminal evaluation events. A learned jump-context network produces an element-wise sigmoid gate and a signed tanh direction; the jump is their product scaled by a maximum bound, added only when the step is terminal.

  • The memory is exactly constant throughout intermediate token construction.
  • Completing a formula ends its Bellman episode but not the memory stream.
  • Jump inputs are the realized pool reward and the categorical evaluation outcome.

Action-conditioned SDE return critic

Conditioned on the encoded partial expression, the pool, the memory and a candidate action, the critic defines a state-action-specific stochastic return process. Euler-Maruyama particles represent plausible future discounted discovery returns. The SDE models search returns, not asset prices.

  • Distinct from the Neural ODE / Neural SDE baselines, which map market features to the target.
  • Dials: number of particles, integration steps, energy-distance weight, maximum diffusion scale.
  • Training uses an energy-distance term, mean calibration, a jump regularizer, and contiguous replay.

Evaluation protocol (read this before believing the gains)

Three Chinese A-share universes (plus CSI1000 in the appendix), one fixed chronological split shared by all methods, h=20 (h=42 for long horizon). All formula-discovery methods share the grammar and a pool capacity of 50; AlphaQCM/AlphaSAGE/AlphaRJM get 10,000 terminal episodes, AlphaGen keeps its native PPO budget (51,200 steps). The wealth curve is a top-20% long-only average of forward returns divided by h — no costs, no shorting, no turnover constraint.

  • Train 2010-01-01 to 2020-12-31; validation 2021; test 2022-01-01 to 2024-12-31.
  • Main table: 4 seeds. Long-horizon: seed 0. CSI1000: seeds 0-1. Ablation: seed 0.
  • Baselines: MLP, LightGBM, Neural ODE, Neural SDE, AlphaGen, AlphaQCM, AlphaSAGE.
  • All experiments ran CPU-only on an Apple M4 Mac mini with 16 GB RAM.

What the ablation isolates

On CSI300 (seed 0) the SDE return critic carries most of the gain. The critic ablation swaps in QCM/IQN rather than a matched-capacity alternative, so 'SDE-ness' and 'richer critic' are not fully separated.

  • Removing the SDE return critic: IC 0.0480 -> 0.0362, ICIR 0.3700 -> 0.2687.
  • Removing persistent memory: 0.0434 / 0.3053. Removing the energy-distance loss: 0.0453 / 0.3085.
  • Sensitivity: fewer particles generally scored better; largely insensitive to integration steps.

Key findings

  • AlphaRJM is best in 10 of 12 dataset-metric cells at h=20 and second in the other two (CSI800 RIC 0.043 vs 0.045; RICIR 0.371 vs 0.396).
  • Absolute edges are small: CSI300 IC 0.042 vs 0.041; clearest gains CSI800 IC 0.036 vs 0.029 and CSI500 ICIR 0.379 vs 0.336 — but CSI500 ICIR std is 0.159, wider than the gap.
  • Long-horizon h=42 (seed 0): AlphaRJM first on all four cells; the plain MLP ties it on CSI300 IC at 0.046.
  • Ablation: removing the SDE return critic costs the most — IC 0.0480 to 0.0362, ICIR 0.3700 to 0.2687.
  • CSI1000: AlphaRJM leads IC (0.076) and RIC (0.102) but has near-lowest ICIR (0.505), beaten by AlphaQCM (0.563) and LightGBM (0.556).
  • Compute footprint is unusually modest — CPU-only on an Apple M4 Mac mini.

Notable quotes

For all datasets, we use a common chronological split, with January 1, 2010–December 31, 2020 for training, January 1, 2021–December 31, 2021 for validation, and January 1, 2022–December 31, 2024 for testing.
The formula-discovery methods share the same expression grammar and an alpha pool capacity of 50.
The w/o SDE Return Critic variant replaces the SDE return-distribution critic with the QCM/IQN return critic while retaining event-conditioned memory, and produces the largest decline in both IC and ICIR.
Fewer particles generally yield stronger correlation metrics, while performance is relatively insensitive to the number of integration steps.

Limitations

  • The cumulative-wealth evidence assumes no transaction costs, slippage or turnover limit — not a tradable backtest.
  • Statistical support is thin outside the main table (single/two seeds), and seed std deviations swamp several reported margins; no significance tests.
  • Training budgets are not normalized across methods, and all evidence is Chinese A-shares over one fixed 2022-2024 window.

Open questions

  • Does the edge survive realistic costs, turnover caps, and a long-short implementation?
  • Why does AlphaRJM lead IC/RIC on CSI1000 yet rank among the weakest on ICIR there?
  • How much of the gain is the SDE parameterization versus simply having any richer critic?
