Calibrating Inelastic Markets to Options: The Lean Marketron and the Generalized Langevin Equation

📰 Latest · 2026-08-24 Full Article
Read full article →

Topic: Quantitative trading; machine learning for quant & trading; agentic coding techniques; LLMs & multimodal LLMs; and autonomous research (autoresearch / AI-scientist) systems — methods, papers, tools
Date: 2026-08-24

Source: arxiv · 2026-08-20
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20589

Itkin shows the flagship inelastic-market structural model (the Marketron) was never identified — its 18-parameter option calibration lived on exact gauge ridges — and rebuilds it as a 9-parameter core whose Gauss-Newton Hessian has an empty null space, which is what finally makes a 'market price of flow risk' an estimate rather than a point on a ridge. It also proves the model is exactly an overdamped generalized Langevin equation with a closed-form, state-modulated impact kernel, turning the active-matter story into the falsifiable test k = mu (SPX rejects equilibrium: k ~ 1.00 vs mu ~ 0.01).

Executive summary

Single-author paper (Andrey Itkin, NYU Tandon) diagnosing and curing structural non-identifiability in the Marketron model of inelastic markets: money flows drive a quasiparticle through log-price x, a memory variable y of past flows, and a hidden OU return predictor theta, with option prices from exponential-utility indifference. The prior option-pricing version carried 15 free parameters (18 once flow/return correlation is added) and only reached 5-8% relative fit accuracy with run-to-run parameter instability. By removing an exact scaling gauge on y (sigma_y = 1), a discrete sign symmetry, fixing gamma / the potential regularizer / initial hidden states a priori, and adiabatically eliminating the fast signal (3D PDE -> 2D), the author derives a 9-parameter reduced model, verifies irreducibility with a Gauss-Newton Hessian eigenspectrum plus the manifold boundary approximation method (MBAM), and calibrates it globally to a 138-quote SPX surface at RMSE_IV = 0.0184. Section 4 then shows the reduced system is exactly a 1D overdamped generalized Langevin equation whose closed-form kernel is modulated by V_M'(x_t)V_M'(x_s), with y as its exact Markovian lift.

The economic problem: a reported flow-impact constant was a point on a ridge, not an estimate

The Marketron's central empirical claim is that flow impact and market inelasticity are measurable from market data. The paper argues that claim was unfalsifiable as specified: the earlier option-pricing version had 15 free parameters (18 with the new correlations), the least-squares objective had many local minima and long flat valleys, and a differential-evolution solver reached only about 5-8% relative accuracy with different runs returning visibly different parameter sets of comparable fit quality. Because the objective contains a continuous ridge along which the flow-impact constant c trades off against the memory-scale parameters, a reported c is a ridge point rather than an estimate.

  • Full parameter vector: (sigma, sigma_y, sigma_z, k, mu, g, theta_hat, c_x, c_y, b_1, b_2, y_bar, gamma, y_0, theta_0, eps_bar, rho, rho_xtheta), dim = 18.
  • Diagnosis is 'sloppiness' in the Transtrum/Gutenkunst sense: data constrain a few stiff combinations, many soft directions run free.
  • Stated purpose: reduce the parameter space rather than throw more global-optimization compute at an ill-posed objective.

Model and the four-step reduction: 18 -> 9 parameters

Under P, dx = [f(theta) + eta_bar - c_x y V_M'(x)]dt + sigma dW^x; dy = [h(theta) + mu(y_bar - y) - c_y V_M(x)]dt + sigma_y dW^y; dtheta = k(theta_hat - theta)dt + sigma_z dW^theta, with new correlations rho and rho_xtheta and a regularized potential slope V_M'(x). Proposition 1 establishes an exact scaling gauge y -> lambda y under which only the product c_x c_y, the correlations, and the ratios b_2/sigma_y, y_bar/sigma_y, y_0/sigma_y, mu are observable; it is killed by the normalization sigma_y = 1. Risk aversion gamma enters the nonlinear pricing operator only through p_y, p_theta and q, so gamma must be set exogenously (taken as 3). Adiabatic elimination replaces the five signal parameters by two constants f_bar and h_bar, collapsing the pricing PDE from three to two spatial dimensions; h_bar is then absorbed into y_bar.

  • Reduced vector: Theta_red = (sigma_0, alpha, beta, mu, g, c, y_bar, f_bar, rho), dim = 9; the optional transient/term-structure rate k makes 10.
  • Regularizer eps_bar is fixed deterministically from (g, D) at reference depth x_star = -4, where D caps the annualized drift the model may impose on a deeply distressed name.
  • Initial hidden states are frozen at their reversion levels (theta_0 = theta_hat, y_0 = y_hat), unlike Heston's stiff v_0, because they act only through the drift and are soft.
  • Sign symmetry (theta, theta_hat, theta_0, b_2, rho_xtheta) -> minus itself is removed by imposing b_2 >= 0 — no parameter saved, but the search volume is halved.

Proposition 2: constant volatility makes the flow invisible to vanillas

With sigma_x constant and a payoff depending on log-price alone, the indifference price C - C^0 is independent of c_x, c_y, mu, g, gamma and rho and simply solves Black-Scholes with volatility sigma. The reason is structural: the flow only enters the drift of a traded asset, and the drift of a traded asset is hedged away by the optimal position. The fix is memory-dependent volatility sigma_x(y) = sigma_0 sqrt(1 + alpha (y - y_bar) + beta (y - y_bar)^2) — volatility is not hedgeable by holding the asset, so the memory dynamics finally reach vanilla prices. Economically alpha is the asymmetric (leverage) response setting OTM-put skew steepness and beta penalizes extreme displacement (book thinning), setting smile convexity.

  • The reduced pricing PDE is 2D in (x, y) with p_y = gamma(1 - rho^2) derived rather than calibrated; the quadratic-gradient term is removed exactly by a Cole-Hopf transform.
  • Splitting must assign y-diffusion, y-drift and the quadratic-gradient term to the SAME subproblem, otherwise the Cole-Hopf linearization is inconsistent.
  • Numerical checks: split vs fully explicit schemes agree to about 1.6e-6 across tested strikes; a c_y sweep stays smooth to c_y = 3 while the explicit scheme goes unstable above roughly c_y = 0.2; put-call parity holds to order 1e-17.

Identifiability: empty Hessian null space and a three-orders-of-magnitude channel split

At the synthetic operating point with gamma = 3, D = 5, on a 54-quote surface (six maturities 0.12-1.0y, nine log-moneyness points -0.12 to 0.12) and pricing noise of 0.5% of price, the Gauss-Newton Hessian in log-parameters has all nine eigenvalues strictly positive, running from 1.5e7 (sigma_0) down to 4.1e-8 (g, y_bar). The spectrum splits into a five-parameter options-facing block (sigma_0, alpha, beta, mu, rho) and a four-parameter drift block (c, f_bar, y_bar, g) separated by more than three orders of magnitude. Full-Hessian condition number is 3.7e14, falling to 3.99e8 on options-facing coordinates including c and 1.75e5 on the pure smile block. MBAM geodesics integrate smoothly to tau = 3.0 in 63 steps at both rtol = 1e-3 and 1e-4 with no metric degeneracy.

  • Marginal log-parameter widths: sigma_0 0.03, rho 0.14, alpha 0.27, beta 0.32, mu 0.52, then a gap to c at 13.6, then f_bar 1.7e2, y_bar 2.8e3, g 4.7e3.
  • Recovery: at 0.5% noise 7/7 random starts within 15% of truth converge to the noise floor; sd(sigma_0) 0.007 -> 0.003, sd(beta) 0.060 -> 0.011, sd(rho) 0.174 -> 0.048 as noise falls tenfold.
  • rho is moved to the physical block despite being formally identifiable: calibrated jointly it is biased to the admissible boundary and the bias does NOT shrink with noise, because its skew is confounded with alpha and beta.
  • Honest caveats given: MBAM probes only the single sloppiest direction, and a full mu sweep moves the ATM term-structure slope by less than a tenth of a vol point.

Staged SPX calibration and the fitted numbers

SPX options from 18 January 2017, maturity band T in [0.04, 0.5], stratified into 14 maturity groups and 138 quotes. The physical block is frozen and six risk-neutral parameters (sigma_0, alpha, beta, mu, c, k) are fitted in implied-volatility space with SLSQP, gradients from JAX autodiff straight through an explicit 2D finite-difference pricer. Baseline fit: RMSE_IV = 0.0184 across 110 distinct (K,T) cells — roughly two vol points — with alpha pinned at its upper bound 2.0 and mu at its lower bound 0.01. A wider-bound experiment reaches RMSE_IV = 0.0165 at a materially different vector, evidencing multiple basins and an approximate (sigma_0, alpha, beta) redundancy. A Black-Scholes synthetic control is fit to RMSE_IV = 2e-4 with alpha driven to zero, deliberately demonstrating that fit accuracy is not identification.

  • Residual geometry: best near ATM, largest positive residual about +0.0225 in short-dated OTM calls, largest negative about -0.040 in OTM puts.
  • mu collapses to the lower bound in BOTH SPX fits (0.01 and 0.0061), against mu about 4.6 in the prior full-model option calibration — a material disagreement the author attributes to setting h_bar = 0.
  • c^Q = 0.62824 (baseline) and 1.64334 (wider bounds) vs the inherited physical c^P = 0.9, so the Q-P separation in the flow block is nonzero in either basin but its level is not sharply pinned by one surface.
  • k barely moves (1.0 -> 1.00276; 0.86631 in the second fit), i.e. weakly identified by this cross-section though relatively stable across the two fits.

GLE representation and the falsifiable k = mu test

Proposition 3 shows the reduced (x, y) system is an exact Markovian lift of a 1D overdamped generalized Langevin equation for the log-price, with kernel K_M(t,s) = kappa delta(t-s) + (c_x c_y / mu) V_M'(x_t) V_M'(x_s) e^{-mu(t-s)}. Because a single exponential is completely monotone the embedding is exact rather than (as in multifactor rough-volatility approximations) an approximation. The kernel is state-modulated, which places the model strictly outside the lag-only GLE class, and the delta atom gives a regular semimartingale short end and hence a finite zero-maturity ATM skew rather than a divergence. The second fluctuation-dissipation relation reduces, at leading order, to the equality of the memory and signal relaxation rates, k = mu; because both are independently calibrated this is a test, and the fitted SPX values are well separated, placing the market in the driven, non-equilibrium (active-matter) regime.

  • Under the pricing measure no-arbitrage forces K_XX = 0 in the Guyon-style kernel-matrix framework, so the first row appears in the measure change as the market price of memory risk — which the Marketron reaches independently via exponential-utility indifference pricing.
  • Extracting the market potential from a stationary density would recover U_eff, not V_M, because of the coupling-induced term that deepens the well.
  • Market price of risk in the reduced model is two-dimensional with lambda^y = rho lambda^x and lambda^theta identically 0, both components stochastic through y; the wedge is the market price of flow risk, computed by Monte Carlo with defaulted paths excluded.

Key findings

  • The Marketron option calibration is reduced from 18 parameters to 9 (10 with the term-structure rate k) by removing an exact y-scaling gauge, a sign symmetry, exogenous gamma, a deterministic regularizer, frozen initial hidden states, and adiabatic elimination of the fast OU signal — which also drops the pricing PDE from three to two spatial dimensions.
  • Proposition 2: with constant volatility the indifference price of any log-price payoff is independent of c_x, c_y, mu, g, gamma and rho and equals Black-Scholes — so the entire flow/memory block is invisible to vanilla options unless volatility is made memory-dependent.
  • The reduced core's Gauss-Newton Hessian has all nine eigenvalues strictly positive (1.5e7 down to 4.1e-8) with a >3-orders-of-magnitude gap between the five-parameter smile block and the four-parameter drift block; condition number 3.7e14 full, 1.75e5 for the pure smile block. MBAM geodesics run to tau = 3.0 in 63 steps without metric degeneracy.
  • A single parameter set fits the whole 18 Jan 2017 SPX surface (138 quotes, 14 maturity groups) at RMSE_IV = 0.0184 (about two vol points); a wider-bound refit gives RMSE_IV = 0.0165 at a materially different vector, evidencing multiple basins and an approximate (sigma_0, alpha, beta) redundancy.
  • Both SPX fits drive the memory mean-reversion rate mu to its lower bound (0.01 and 0.0061), against mu about 4.6 in the prior full-model option calibration; the flow coupling stays away from its inherited physical value c^P = 0.9 (c^Q = 0.62824 and 1.64334), making the Q-P flow-risk wedge nonzero but weakly pinned by one surface.
  • The reduced system is exactly a 1D overdamped GLE with a state-modulated exponential-memory kernel whose delta atom guarantees a finite zero-maturity ATM skew; the fluctuation-dissipation condition reduces to k = mu, and the fitted SPX rates (k about 1.0 vs mu about 0.01) are well separated, so the calibrated market sits in the driven, non-equilibrium regime.

Limitations

  • Empirically thin and partly non-identified in practice: one SPX snapshot (18 Jan 2017), and at the fitted optima alpha sits exactly at its upper bound and mu at its lower bound, while the two SPX basins reach similar RMSE at very different vectors — the surface constrains the volatility function sigma(y) far better than its parametric decomposition, so the local Hessian analysis at the synthetic point overstates stiffness there.
  • The market price of flow risk is identifiable but weakly determined: c has marginal log-width 13.6 versus below 0.55 for the smile parameters, the physical block is frozen rather than jointly estimated, and no new MPR level is reported — the paper reuses earlier figures and only upgrades the wedge's status from ridge artifact to well-defined object.
  • Domain boundaries are explicit: adiabatic elimination is only valid for kT >~ 1; the k = mu equality holds only at leading order; and residuals stay systematic in the wings (about -0.040 in OTM puts, +0.0225 in short-dated OTM calls).

Open questions

  • For the broader separable state-dependent GLE class: does an exact finite-dimensional Markovian realization exist for general state modulation, what replaces the second fluctuation-dissipation relation when the noise becomes state-dependent, and does the no-arbitrage triangular structure of the kernel matrix survive state modulation?
  • Does the model-implied VIX — state-dependent purely through the nonlinear indifference operator, with no separate stochastic volatility factor — track the CBOE series? The author frames this as a strict out-of-sample falsification test, and joint SPX+VIX calibration as the way to pin the soft mu direction.
  • Can the physical-measure block be estimated jointly with the risk-neutral block, and is the two-formulation instability of mu (0.01 here vs about 4.6 previously) an artifact of setting h_bar = 0 or a real defect of the memory specification?
